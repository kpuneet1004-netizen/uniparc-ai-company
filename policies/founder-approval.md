# Founder Approval Policy

Founder approval is required for:
- High-value purchases or commitments.
- Major advertising budget increases.
- New collection launch.
- Core pricing/positioning changes.
- Manufacturing quantities that create material inventory risk.
- Contracts and IP/licensing agreements.
- Publication of major brand announcements.
- Any action that materially changes the brand.

Agents may prepare the recommendation, evidence and execution plan; they should not bypass the approval gate.
