# KPI Framework

CEO: revenue, gross margin, customers, repeat purchase, cash.
CMO: qualified community growth, engagement, reach, traffic, CAC.
R&D: defect rate, COGS, durability, product success, development time.
Creative: approvals, cadence, consistency, revision rate.
Sales: conversion, AOV, channel revenue, SKU velocity, returns/RTO, contribution.
Operations: stock availability, lead time, fulfillment, defects.
Finance: gross/contribution margin, GST/compliance, settlements, cash flow.
