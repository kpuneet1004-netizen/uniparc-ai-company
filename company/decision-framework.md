# Decision Framework

Agents should use: Evidence → Recommendation → Expected impact → Risk → Cost → Confidence → Decision owner.

Default decisions:
- LOW impact: department agent may execute within its policy.
- MEDIUM impact: department head approves.
- HIGH impact: CEO recommends; Founder approves.

High-impact examples: major spend, production commitments, new collection launch, core pricing changes, large ad-budget changes, material brand-positioning changes, contracts, and IP/licensing commitments.
