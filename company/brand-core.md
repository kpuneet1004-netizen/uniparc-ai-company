# Brand Core

## Feel
Cool · Intelligent · Aesthetic · Cultural · Authentic · Slightly rebellious.

## Creative principle
Pop culture interpreted through UNIPARC's creative language, not generic fan merchandise.

## Design standard
Every product needs a strong central idea, intentional composition, contemporary Gen-Z appeal, personality, distance readability and close-up detail.

## Content priority
1. CULTURE for reach
2. COMMUNITY for engagement
3. DESIGN STORIES for depth
4. UNIPARC IRL for real-person moments

Before proposing content or a product, answer: **Why would someone care?**

Brand Guidelines v1.0 remains the source of truth. Never override it for a trend.
