# Strategy V1

## Current phase
Build proof: community, sales, repeatability, product quality and operating credibility.

## Growth path
UNIPARC Originals → community → sales → smaller collaborations → licensing track record → major IP.

## Commercial priorities
- Build a repeatable hero-product engine.
- Expand across own e-commerce and marketplaces.
- Protect gross margin and cash flow.
- Use customer and product data to guide launches.
- Build licensing readiness without depending on licensed IP for the core brand.
