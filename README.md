# UNIPARC AI Company

UNIPARC AI Company is an agent-operated business system designed around a founder-controlled AI organization.

## Operating model
- Founder / Board: final authority on strategy, spend, product launches, major pricing changes, and IP/licensing.
- CEO: converts company goals into priorities, delegates work, reviews results, and escalates decisions.
- Departments: CMO, R&D, Creative, Sales, Operations, Finance.
- Human approval is required for high-impact actions.

## North-star
Build UNIPARC into a profitable, culture-first Indian Gen-Z apparel brand with strong community, repeatable sales, distinctive original products, operational excellence, and the credibility to secure major IP partnerships.

See `company/`, `agents/`, `skills/`, and `policies/` for the operating system.
